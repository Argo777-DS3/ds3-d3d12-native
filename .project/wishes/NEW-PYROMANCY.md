# Wish: a new pyromancy

A wish of Thomas (project chat, November 2026), copied here from the coordinator's notes on 2026-11-12 (D-0273, D-0214). Not a
decision and not a workstream of the [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) (its workstream 21 lists the wishes): no thread without
Thomas's go. The wording below is the coordinator's summary of his idea, not his words.

## The spell

- The caster curls up (the charge), then throws the arms and the chest forward: the flame comes out of the thorax.
- A huge halo of flames around the caster, lasting ten seconds or more, that hurts the enemies close by. Base: Chaos Bed
  Vestiges.
- A gust of wind and a camera shake at the release; a new cry.
- A second version with black flames.

## The look

- Inspiration: the Phoenix of Final Fantasy XVI, for the mood only; the style stays Dark Souls III's.
