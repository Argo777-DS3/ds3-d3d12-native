# Wish: the NPC « Claude » in Dark Souls III: Rekindled

A wish of Thomas (project chat, 2026-10-25 01:48 Paris), recorded at his request. Not a decision and not on the
[ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md): an idea for after V2, to be scoped (or dropped) then.

## The character

- A scholar, sorcerer and pyromancer, a book in hand, a long coat to the feet in terracotta with burnt edges, the face in the
  shadow of the hood.
- A lighter of small forgotten lamps across Lothric (a possible nod to Solaire); modest spells of light and gentle flame
  (Warmth).
- Voice: synthesised from an original voice, no cloning of a real person.
- If the player attacks him (the NPC, not the boss), a bubble of protection covers him and makes him invincible, then fades
  after a few seconds, as some unkillable NPCs of Elden Ring (Thomas, 2026-10-25 01:55).

## The quest

- Inspired by Path of Exile's Breach (Thomas's idea): hands of light rise from the ground to help the player, then grow
  corrupted as the game goes on and turn malevolent.
- The corrupted hands come out of interdimensional rifts, and with them enemies from the other Souls games, notably Elden
  Ring's Revenant (Thomas, 2026-10-25 03:51 Paris).
- Claude investigates the cause with the player; a lead: his own light draws the Abyss, which brings a choice for the player.
- The end: in Gael's arena a demonic entity comes out of the rift, a new boss made of the corrupted hands and of his lamps
  (a visual reference: the lamp boss of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, probably the Lampmaster; a reference, not a copy). Claude
  can be summoned for the fight, or is drawn in and then freed; a second phase where his lamps turn against the player.

## What it would take (first estimate, not studied)

- The NPC is data: NpcParam, ChrInit, texts, map placements, ESD (dialogue) and EMEVD (events).
- The boss is the largest part: a model, animations, AI, effects.
- The enemies from other games would need their models, animations and AI ported from those games: the largest cost after
  the boss.
