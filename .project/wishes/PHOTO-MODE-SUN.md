# Wish: a photo mode that moves the sun

An idea outside the plan (D-0298) the study thread offered with the study ahead of workstream 12
([SYNTHESE](../studies/workstream-12/SYNTHESE.md), 2026-11-22), and Thomas's answer in that thread (2026-11-22 15:32 Paris): « Et
oui ton idée hors plan est bonne ». Not a decision on when; listed in [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) workstream 21, nothing in front of
the open workstreams.

- What: a photo mode in which the player moves the zone's sun (the hour of the day); the shadows, the ambient and the fog follow.
- Why it becomes cheap: once workstream 12 has made the sky a real source and the fog a lit medium (D-0307), nothing in the
  traced lighting is tied to the authored sun direction but the sun itself and the sky's picture.
- What it needs beyond workstream 12: the game paused with a free camera (not studied), a row or a key for the sun's angle, and
  a rule for the drawn sky, whose dome is a picture painted for one hour (its colour and strength under a moved sun: to study).
- Not before workstream 12 is done, and not before Thomas's go for a thread.
