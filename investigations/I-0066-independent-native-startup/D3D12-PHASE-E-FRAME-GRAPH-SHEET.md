## 0. Answer first

1. **Two reprises, two very different urgencies.** R10 (a frame graph that owns resource states and barriers) is the **gate of the coming families**: the game path hand-writes 51 `Transition` call sites in its encoders (section 1), and shadows, AO, YEBIS (about 100 draw nodes), glow and effects would add several hundred more by hand. R11 (aliased transient targets) is a **memory optimisation whose need is not shown yet**: on the test adapter (RTX 4070 Ti SUPER) the native render-target set is about 100 MB at 1280x720 of 1.8 GB of local video memory in use; aliasing saves about 39 MB today and about 69 MB with the planned families at 720p, but about 350 MB today and 625 MB planned at 4K (section 4). Build R10 first, measure (E6), then decide R11.
2. **Inventory (sections 2 and 3).** 16 pass slots in the planned single frame, **9 of which exist natively** (G-buffer, environment regions, the three lighting slots, forward, velocity, UI, composition and present), plus the movie and the menu views in front; **27 modelled transient targets** (and the library-derived pyramids, outside the per-pixel model) and about ten persistent or external ones. Byte sizes per pixel are exact for the formats in the code; the lifetimes are the order of the master plan section 2.
3. **Aliasing potential (section 4):** today's transient set is 96 bytes per pixel if every target is separate and 52 if lifetimes share storage (-46 %); the planned frame is 147 against 68 (-54 %); a greedy placement reaches the peak in both. With Heap Tier 1 the render-target class (127 against 60) and the non-render-target class (20 against 20) need separate heaps (80 in all, -46 %). Never aliased: the shadow atlases (128 MiB plus 32 MiB), the temporal history family, **the scene colour copy** (it is the water/effects "FrameCopy" and a real cross-frame read), the swap chain, ingress target records.
4. **The frame today depends on conventions that the graph must make data (section 6):** every transient returns to a fixed home state at the end of the frame; the depth resource changes only its plane 0 state while its stencil plane stays writable (so a pass may name the same resource twice with two plane states, which today's validator refuses); several lighting targets are overwritten by full-screen draws without a clear (legal for committed resources, **not** for an aliased render target); the 1x1 neutral images are cleared and toggled every frame; barrier *placement* today is coarser than a minimal plan (all six G-buffers go to shader-readable at the lighting entry), so the shadow-mode criterion is equality of state **at each use**, not equality of barrier lists.
5. **Order (section 7):** E0 measurement; E1 `FramePlan` extension (pure code, unit-tested); E2 shadow mode; E3 conversion pass by pass behind `frame-graph.flag`; E4 batching; E5 cross-frame initial states; E6 the memory decision; E7 to E9 placed heaps, aliasing barriers and discard discipline, views sharing the main view's storage, behind `alias-targets.flag`; E10 the M-run. Phase D's view contexts and single list (cD2, cD3) precede E3 for the views and E9.
6. **What this sheet does not do:** reorder passes (R10's rule), design the families' own graphs (their notes do), or touch the UI filter targets (already reused by life inside `PrimitivePass`).

## 4. Lifetimes and alias potential (derived)

Method: the first and last slot of each transient (section 3.1) give an interval; the peak of the live B/px over the slots is the lower bound of any heap that realises the plan; a greedy placement by decreasing size is compared with the peak; the same for the two Heap Tier 1 classes. The per-pixel model is an idealisation (the fractional targets are scaled to full-resolution equivalents, the library pyramids and the water reflection view are not counted; real sizes come from `GetResourceAllocationInfo`, with 64 KB placement alignment).

Persistent targets, the scene copy (8 B/px) and the swap chain (8 B/px) add about 16 B/px in both. Plus, per menu-view extent, a full `TargetSet` (76 B/px at that extent: colours 32, depth 8, lighting 24, env 4, SSS 8) kept until the device closes; the number of distinct extents at the status page and character creation is OPEN (E0).

Reading: (1) the saving is real but **small in absolute terms at the reference resolution** and large only at 1440p and above; (2) the long-lived targets set the peak: GB5 (until glow), the depth (until velocity or DoF), the lit HDR (until YEBIS) and the G-buffer colours through lighting; (3) the peak sits at slots 7 and 8 (lighting), where the planned frame holds G-buffer + `AoFull` + mask + `DepthRead` + the two accumulators; (4) the greedy layout reaches the peak because the lifetimes nest well, but real sizes differ and the margin must be recomputed from allocation info.

### 4.1 Alias sets worth recording (planned frame, one RT/DS heap)

Disjoint pairs and groups the greedy placement actually uses: {GB0, SSS temporary, lit HDR, glow source}; {env, `AoDepthHalf`, `AoRaw`} next to {light0, light1}; {light2, velocity, motion vectors, `Ldr[A]`}; {`YScene`, `Ldr[B]`, UI depth} at the end; `DepthRead`, GB0 copy and GB1 copy form the non-RT class (the scene copy is not among them). **These are illustrations of the planner's output, not a layout to hard-code**: the planner computes offsets from the compiled lifetimes (section 5).

### 4.2 What must never be placed on top of what

Anything with a cross-frame edge (history, the scene copy, the atlas) and anything read after the last pass of the frame. Today only the back buffer is read after the last pass: the velocity, view and movie readbacks happen inside their own pass (`:1276-1286`, `:1400`, `:1515`), so witness sampling extends no transient's life yet (G6 stays for the passes that will read later).

## 5. Design

### 5.1 Data (proposed names; `frame_plan.h` stays API-free)

- `FrameResourceDesc { ResourceKey key; uint32_t width,height,depth_or_array,mips; DXGI_FORMAT format; uint32_t flags (render target, depth stencil, unordered access, copy only); Lifetime lifetime (Transient, Persistent, External); D3D12_RESOURCE_STATES home; ClearValue clear; uint32_t planes; }`. `External` = swap chain buffers and ingress records: the graph never allocates them, and **their boundary states are declared inputs of the plan** (class convention: back buffer `PRESENT`; ingress targets `PIXEL|NON_PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE`; scene copy `PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE`; persistent history their declared state), a mismatch is a `PlanError`, so a cached compiled plan stays **immutable** (the R13 rule V1) and carries no mutable state across frames. The back buffer slot (index 0 or 1) is an execution parameter, not a plan resource, so a cached plan holds no reference to a swap-chain buffer and does not block `ResizeBuffers`.
- `PassResource` gains `required_state` and a `SubresourceRange`, with the vocabulary the notes ask for plus the combined states the code uses: `DEPTH_WRITE`, `DEPTH_READ|PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE` per plane, read-only DSV, `RENDER_TARGET`, `PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE`, `PIXEL|NON_PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE`, `COPY_SOURCE/DEST`, `UNORDERED_ACCESS`, `PRESENT`.
- **`DuplicateUse` is redefined as an overlap of subresource ranges for the same key** (today any second naming is refused, `frame_plan.cpp:53`): the environment regions and the SSS read plane 0 as a shader resource and use the read-only DSV with the stencil plane writable (`:1145-1150`, `:1170`, `:1017`), which are two uses of one resource in disjoint planes.
- `PassKind::PostProcess`; caps raised to 256 passes **and 256 `after` entries** (the second cap, `:31`) with the `plan_passes_peak` counter; a pass may carry a **draw-node list** that the validator does not expand.
- Output of the compiler (pure, unit-testable): per pass a `BarrierBatch { transitions[], aliasing[], uav[] }` computed by a `StateTracker` (state per resource and subresource, declared boundary states, `home` at the end), the first-use action per transient (`Clear`, `Discard`, `CopyInit`, `None`), and a `PlacementPlan { heap_class, size, per-resource offset }` from `Lifetimes()` (greedy by decreasing size over `GetResourceAllocationInfo` sizes, 64 KB alignment, persistent and external excluded).
- **Executor:** `PlanExecutor::Run(list, compiled, encoders)` emits each pass's barrier batch with one `ResourceBarrier` call (ER E8) and then the pass's encoder callback; encoders stop calling `Transition`. Conversion is pass by pass (E3), so a hybrid exists.
- **Cache key (corrected):** the *structure* of the frame, not its draw counts: the group list and the presence flags that change structure (lighting present, SSS present, forward rows, point lights, velocity, scene copy, UI packet, water/effects), the extent and `target_generation_`, and the witness flags that lengthen lifetimes. Draw counts and draw-node lists are excluded (they change neither barriers nor placement); the master-plan digest row C0 (which lists draw counts) stays a *witness* row, not the key. Built on the owner thread from immutable inputs and executed by whichever thread records.

### 5.2 Rules (G1 to G8)

- **G1:** the graph preserves the original pass order; it may reorder independent passes only after E3's witnesses pass, and E3 forbids it.
- **G2:** a transient returns to its home state at the end of its activation (today's "left in render target"). Activation order: aliasing barrier, then the transition to the state the initialisation needs (`RENDER_TARGET` or `DEPTH_WRITE` for clear and discard, `COPY_DEST` for a copy), then the initialisation, then the uses.
- **G3:** a render-target or depth-stencil placed texture must be cleared, discarded or copied into before any other use **after `CreatePlacedResource` and after each aliasing activation** (a D3D12 requirement for such textures, to be confirmed under the debug layer and GPU-based validation, E8); the initialising clear covers the whole subresource (no rectangles) and, for a depth-stencil resource, both planes; a full-screen overwrite is not an initialisation.
- **G4:** no placed resource is created or destroyed per frame: resources are created at the generation change, their views stay valid, only barriers and first-use actions are per frame.
- **G5:** persistent and external resources are tracked, never placed; a history needs `initialized = true` for its previous identity and its own identity for the current one.
- **G6:** a witness readback that happens after a resource's last pass is a plan use that extends its lifetime; the plan key contains the witness flags.
- **G7:** on one queue the heap and its resources are shared by the frame slots of phase D; the first activation of a frame carries an aliasing barrier against the previous frame's last use (list order is queue order). A second queue (phase G) needs per-queue placement.
- **G8:** every plan failure is a refusal at plan time (`PlanError`), never a runtime barrier guess.

### 5.3 Shadow-mode criterion (E2) and conversion granularity (E3)

The hand-written code is **coarser** than a minimal plan: all six G-buffers go to `PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE` at the lighting entry (`:1082`) although GB2, GB4 and GB5 are first read later (`:1006-1010`), and all return together at the end (`:1127`). A minimal planner puts those barriers elsewhere, so a comparison of barrier lists would never reach zero. **Criterion:** for every pass and every resource subresource, the state the graph holds at the pass's use equals the state the hand-written code has at that use (as recorded by the wrapped `Transition()` calls), and the state at the end of the frame equals the expected home/boundary state; barrier placement and count are free (E4 measures the reduction). `graph_barrier_mismatch` rows report (pass, resource, subresource, expected state, actual state).

## 6. Pitfalls (with the evidence in this code)

1. **Depth planes and the planes' uses.** `DepthPlaneTransition` moves subresource 0 only (`scene_pass.cpp:70-74`): plane 0 toggles `DEPTH_WRITE` and `DEPTH_READ|PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE` (`:1083-1126`, `:1268-1275`) while the stencil plane stays writable for the SSS stencil test through the read-only DSV (`dsv_` slot 1, depth read-only flag). A whole-resource state model gets this wrong; a barrier on `ALL_SUBRESOURCES` is invalid while the planes hold different states. The graph needs plane subresource ranges and the redefined `DuplicateUse` (5.1). An option to consider: a DSV with `READ_ONLY_DEPTH|READ_ONLY_STENCIL` would make both planes `DEPTH_READ` for the environment regions and the SSS stage, which do not write the stencil.
2. **Clear values and the depth's stencil.** Every committed target is created with an optimised clear value of its own (`Targets()` per format; velocity 0.5, 0.5, 1.0, 0; depth 1 and a stencil). The depth's clear stencil is `input.clear_stencil` (`:1580`), which defaults to 0 and is never assigned in the game path (complement 4.1); the resource is recreated (after a `Drain`, `++target_generation_`) if it ever changes (`:1362`, `:1564-1567`) precisely so that the clear matches the creation value. **`Diagnostics()` counts warnings as errors** (`:782`, `Severity<=WARNING`), so a clear that does not match the creation value fails the frame under the debug layer. For placed resources each resource keeps its own creation clear value (aliased resources may differ, the rule is the ordinary one: clear with the value of *that* resource); a stencil change is a generation change and a placed recreation (compatible with G4).
3. **Overwrite is not initialisation.** light0 to light3 and the motion vectors are written by full-screen draws without a clear (`pass(0..3)`, `:1108-1121`; the velocity resolve): correct for committed resources, incorrect for aliased render targets: E8 adds `DiscardResource` (or a clear) as the first-use action.
4. **The 1x1 neutral images** are cleared white and black and flipped between `RT` and `SRV` every frame (`:1085-1086`, `:1124`): persistent, tiny, and exactly the kind of hand-written toggle the graph should own (declared boundary state, E5).
5. **Home-state conventions** ("left readable between frames", PREP D-G E5): the lighting leaves the lit HDR shader-readable (`:1122`) and the composition returns it to `RENDER_TARGET` (`:1615`; the view path does it at `:1407`); the G-buffer returns to `RENDER_TARGET` at the end of the lighting (`:1127`), the depth to `DEPTH_WRITE` (`:1126`). They become declared boundary states (E5); the first frame after a (re)creation starts from the creation state (`RENDER_TARGET`/`DEPTH_WRITE` for committed targets; the scene copy `PIXEL_SHADER_RESOURCE`).
6. **The scene copy** is the one cross-frame GPU read today (the views drawn before the main view bind the previous frame's copy, `game_bootstrap.cpp:3985`, `:4082`); it is persistent, not a transient, and not part of the 147 B/px.
7. **Witness readbacks** (back buffer every 120th frame; velocity and motion every 30th; view and movie pictures) are the only reads after the last pass for the back buffer; the others are inside their passes today (4.2).
8. **Menu views.** Each view runs its own G-buffer, lighting and forward (V) in a `TargetSet` of its extent, swapped in by `SwapTargetSet` (18 member swaps): the graph needs phase D's `ViewContext` (cD2) first. The view target and the movie target are ingress records (external, combined shader state outside the pass). A shared transient set across views (E9) needs the views' lifetimes disjoint in the list (they are sequential) and the largest extent or per-extent placed resources in one heap.
9. **Frames in flight** (phase D): one heap shared by the slots is valid on one queue (G7); the CPU records frame N+1's resource uses while frame N is still executing, so barriers are in list order and plan state is per recorded frame; the immutable-plan rule (5.1) keeps this safe across threads.
10. **Heap Tier.** Not logged today. Tier 1 forbids mixing render-target/depth textures, non-RT textures and buffers in one heap (section 4: 80 against 68 B/px). `DepthRead` and the GB copies are copy destinations (non-RT class); `Lut3D` and `DofTable` are UAV textures (non-RT, persistent).
11. **Alignment and size.** Placement offsets are multiples of the resource alignment (64 KB for these textures; 4 MB if a multisampled target ever appears); sizes come from `GetResourceAllocationInfo`, not from B/px.
12. **Resize and failure.** A new extent recreates heap, resources and views after the ring-wide `WaitIdle` (phase D), resets the histories (R-T5) and keeps the old heap until its ticket; `CreateHeap` can fail at 4K (a 400 to 540 MB heap): fall back to committed resources per group, never refuse the frame.
13. **UI targets** are already reused by life inside `PrimitivePass` (a packet target is one *use*, pooled by size, SCALEFORM-FILTERS-NATIVE F1 revised) and are render-target memory the figures above omit; they stay outside the graph until the UI becomes graph-aware (optional extension of E9), and the UI depth/stencil should become persistent per extent first (phase D P1).
14. **Descriptors** are not graph state, but their lifetime follows the resource's: views of transients are created once per generation (G4); the shader-visible light view heap is per `TargetSet` (`:1337`), another reason the views need their own contexts.
15. **Debug layer and GBV.** Aliasing errors are silent on hardware; one run with GPU-based validation on the aliased build is part of the exit (E8). GBV must be enabled with `SetEnableGPUBasedValidation` **before the device is created**, so E8 touches the device creation (`DeviceQueues::Open`, `backend.cpp`) and adds a flag; the validation layer serialises, so timing is judged without it.
16. **Plan cost.** `CompileFramePlan` allocates `P x P` boolean matrices and vectors per call (O(P x D x P)); with 100 to 200 nodes per frame it is acceptable once, not per frame: cache by the structure key (5.1), and keep the compile off the worker's critical path.
17. **ER** shows barrier records of kind transition, aliasing and UAV and one `ResourceBarrier` per group (E8), and optional placed buffers (E7); nothing shows aliased render targets in ER, so the aliasing design here is DS3's own, validated by the tests below.
18. **Planned passes missing from the per-pixel model:** the water reflection view (640x360 HDR + depth, NATIVE-PASS-ORDER step 2), the second depth downsample (step 11), the velocity dilation (group 0x35), a second mask target when the shadow blur is on, and a re-copy of `DepthRead` after the water: they add short-lived targets inside slots 2 to 12 and may lower or raise the peak; E1's inventory is updated from the family notes when they land.
19. **Numbering.** PHASES-D-G-PREP 2.6 calls E6 the measurement, E7 the placed arena, E8 aliasing and E9 the shared views; this sheet adds E0 (the rows) before them, E1 to E5 for R10, and keeps E6 as the decision gate (its measurement is E0's rows).

## 9. Risks and open points

1. **Silent aliasing errors** are the reason for the order: shadow mode first, pass-by-pass conversion, aliasing last, one GBV run (master-plan risk 1).
2. **Value of R11 is not shown** on this adapter (E6); the planned families are what make it worth having at 1440p and 4K, and the shadow atlases (160 MiB persistent) dwarf the aliasable set at 720p.
3. **Lifetimes of the planned families are the notes' orders, not measurements;** the greedy numbers move with the real sizes (library-derived pyramids, DoF layers, the passes of item 18) and with the first real passes.
4. **OPEN:** `ResourceHeapTier` of the adapters in use (E0); whether the intended output resolution is above 1080p; the number of menu-view extents (E0); the order of the movie and the views against the scene (section 2); a WARP-capable lighting fixture for T-E2; whether upscaler consumers will keep the motion vectors past the frame (they would leave the transient class); who owns the UI depth once it is persistent (phase D P1).
5. **Scope:** compute/UAV transients and the second queue (phase G) are not designed here; G7 notes what they would change.

**E4 (batching).** Met by the executor's form: each pass boundary records the previous pass's after-batch and the next pass's before-batch in one `ResourceBarrier` call, so an in-game frame takes 13 calls for 40 transitions (Native1444) against 47 calls of one barrier each by hand; a further merge across a pass boundary would move a barrier over the pass's commands and is not done.

**E5 (declared boundary states).** The neutral images (the white t32/t33 and the black image) never change: they are cleared once by the first lighting recording after their creation and stay shader-readable between frames (they were cleared and toggled every recording: two clears and four transitions saved per lighting recording). The graph learns this through a declared start state (`FrameResourceDesc::start`, `GraphShadow::DeclareStarting`: the first recording starts from the creation state, every recording ends in the boundary state; T-E1 checks both). The other "left readable" conventions are already the declared home and boundary states of the executed plans (the lit HDR, the G-buffer, the depth planes, the view and movie targets, the back buffer).
