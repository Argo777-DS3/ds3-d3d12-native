# The engine's delayed deletion: what `DelayDeleteStep` frees, when, on which thread, and what it imposes on the native retirement hooks and on F2 (static audit)

Request (coordinator, 2026-10-11 20:01 UTC, proposition A after [ENGINE-STEP-THREAD-MAP](ENGINE-STEP-THREAD-MAP.md)): audit `DelayDeleteStep` and the retirement hooks: what is deleted, when, relative to `EndFrame`, and what this imposes on the kept-set rule of [phase F](D3D12-PHASE-F-SHARED-STATE-SHEET.md). Static reading of the Ghidra mirror and of the native code; nothing was run or measured, and no retained log covers deletion. An independent Opus 5.5 pass corrected the first version (the uncounted queue is not a file-cap or model-data queue; delays are not all 1; the pose-palette retirement is safe); this version carries those corrections. Grades: **STATIC**, **INFERRED**, **OPEN**.

## 0. Answer first

1. **One step, two queues, flushed together.** `DelayDeleteStep` (0x5E, group `g1`, lane 0, **row 85 of 88**, after `EndFrame` and before `RecordHeapStats`/`FrameEnd`) has an update that flushes (a) the generic **counted** delayed-delete heap `[144964930]` (`FUN_142355A10`) and then (b) the `DelayDeleteManImp` queue `[14477D778]` (`FUN_140819450`). Its init (`FUN_140EE41E0`) creates both singletons and its finish (`FUN_140EE4140`) destroys them. The attribution of these three functions to the step's Init/Update/Finish states is **INFERRED (strong)**: the dispatcher `FUN_140EE3BC0` calls `[this+0x10] + state*0x10`, but that table (`14489DA30`) is zero in the image and filled at run time; the evidence is the strings `DelayDeleteStep::STEP_Init/Update/Finish`, the globals the three functions share, and their behaviour (`Update` advances only if `FUN_140F0F4F0(this)` holds, `Finish` writes -1, the terminal state).
2. **Counted heap: delay d means deletion at the (d+1)-th flush after the add.** An entry holds `{deleter, object, counter}`; each flush decrements and deletes when the counter is below zero (`FUN_142355A10`). The delay is chosen per deleter (section 2): 1 for most, 2, 5, 6 and 10 for a few. With delay 1, an object queued before row 85 of frame N is deleted at row 85 of frame N+1.
3. **The uncounted `DelayDeleteManImp` queue is a behaviour-resource queue.** Its only producer is `FUN_1408193F0`, whose only caller `FUN_140DAA7C0` is reached from `BehbndResCap#1` (`FUN_140E06650`); its purge `FUN_140DE14A0` drops one reference of the queued cap through the HKX repository `[144794FA8]` (`FUN_1423563D0`; freed only when the count reaches zero), subtracts a memory budget (`FUN_140E44F00`) and destroys the item. It holds **no model data**; items are processed at the next flush (row 85 of the same frame when queued earlier in the frame).
4. **No static link was found between `DelayDeleteStep` and the native model-storage retirement.** `RetireModelStorage` (`A05C0`) is reached from `GXFlverModelData#1`, the destructor of `SprjFD4FlverModelData` (`140D35E60` -> `140D35CD0` -> `14009FF50`), its slot 3 (`1400A0410`) and the reload path (`1400A0430` from slot 4 `1400A0170`). The deferred path that touches model resources is `FD4FileCapDeleter` (added from `ChrAsmModelRes`, `FUN_140C422D0`/`FUN_140C42620`, counted); what its delete slot releases was not read. **OPEN.**
5. **The pose retirement is safe; the F2 risk is the ticket of the retirement.** `RetireNativeModelPose` only calls `ModelPoseOwner::Withdraw()` (`native/content/model_pose.cpp:126`: `withdrawn_=true; current_.reset();`) and the collect copies a `shared_ptr<const NativeModelPoseSnapshot>` that keeps its palette version alive (`game_bootstrap.cpp:4339-4340`, `:1937`), so a job holding that snapshot is unaffected. What is **not** safe by construction is `RetireWhenSubmittedWorkCompletes` (`native/renderer/backend.cpp:779-782`), used by the stream (`:1787`) and storage (`DestroyModelStorage`, `:6333`) retirements: it takes `LastSubmitted()` **at the call**. Under F2, a retirement at row 85 of frame N, while job N has not submitted yet, receives the ticket of frame N-1 and the object can be released before job N's own commands complete, unless job N holds its own `shared_ptr` to what it uses (`selected.geometry`, `selected.streams`, ...) until its own ticket. That rule is the recommendation of section 4.
6. **Design assumption, not a code fact:** that `Post(N+1)` waits for job N (depth-1 queue) is the R13 sheet's design.

The twenty deleter objects (`144596990..144596A28`) are static and get their vtables in initialisers near `142672xxx`. Two further adds bypass the manager lock: `FUN_14238C720` (the destructor of an object of vtable `143D7A5F0`, called from `FUN_140335170`) and `FUN_1423C5610`, both with the deleter `1447416A8` (vtable `143D7D2B0`) and delay 1. The flush takes no lock, and an add made by a deleter during the flush is appended to the list and decremented in the same pass.

**`Unref` is not a plain delete.** The add functions `FUN_140818F30` (`GXNonAtomicReferenceCountObject`) and `FUN_1408192B0` (`GXSgObject`) **increment `+8` without an interlocked operation, before taking the lock, on the adding thread**; the flush decrements (`FUN_140818760` for the non-atomic object, `FUN_140818780` for `GXSgObject`, which calls the object's slot 0 and frees at zero). So the call keeps the object alive for `delay` more flushes, and the two refcount writes happen on two different threads in general.

**`DelayDeleteManImp` `[14477D778]`** (a vector at `mgr+8`, lock at `queue+0x20`): the only producer is `FUN_1408193F0`, called only by `FUN_140DAA7C0` <- `FUN_140E06590` <- `BehbndResCap#1` (`FUN_140E06650`); the item is `[obj+0x38]`. The flush `FUN_140819450` copies the vector out under the lock (`FUN_140817480`), empties and shrinks it (`FUN_1408199B0`, `FUN_140819A50`), releases the lock and processes each item with `FUN_140DE14A0`: `FUN_1423563D0` drops one reference of the item (`+0x58`; frees only at zero with `+0x5C <= 0`), `FUN_140E44F00([144798338], item[0x24])` subtracts a four-counter memory budget, then the item is destroyed through its slot 1. `[144794FA8]` is read by `ChrbndFileCap#6` and `HkxFileCap#6/#7`: it is the HKX repository. A `BehbndResCap` destroyed during the counted flush feeds this vector, which is processed in the same `Update` because it follows the counted flush.

**A second family on the GX side:** `GXDelayDeleter` and `_Free`, `_Unref<GXNonAtomicReferenceCountObject>`, `_Unref<CGResource>`, `_Delete<CGResource|CGView|CGBuffer|CGConstantBuffer|CGPredicate|GXSssProfileManager>` (RTTI `143D4F830`, `143D5B7D8..`); vtables are set in small constructors (`14019AC10..14019AD60`, `140112D20/D50`). The location and delay of its flush were not found. **OPEN**, and whether it holds only CPU wrappers under the native renderer is not shown.

## 4. What to do (pilot-facing, technical)

1. **Job-held references and tickets (cR2/cR3, kept-set):** the F2 job holds by `shared_ptr` everything captured at collect (`selected.geometry`, `selected.streams`, pose snapshot, texture records) until its own ticket completes, **or** retirement uses `RetireAfter(max(LastSubmitted(), ticket reserved for the job in flight))`. One of the two is required once record runs on the worker; the current `LastSubmitted()` rule is correct only while submission is synchronous with the retirement caller.
2. **Weak texture references under F2:** audit `game_bootstrap.cpp:521-523` (the selection is held weakly) against `RetireTexture` and the registry retire; either make the job hold the record or document why a stale selection is harmless.
3. **Non-atomic GX refcounts:** the add side increments on the adding thread and the flush decrements on the owner thread. Native code must not touch the `+8` count of `GXSgObject`/`GXNonAtomicReferenceCountObject` from the job; the collect copies what it needs today. Keep it so (a note for the cF.5 allow-list).
4. **Passive row TM-6 (pilot's cR0):** at each retirement hook log the thread, **the return address of the caller** (to tell the destructor, slot 3 and reload callers apart) and whether a render job is in flight. Before F1 the "in flight" flag is 0 by construction (`Clear()` is synchronous inside `EndFrame`); the row only measures after F1.
5. **Open items (B and later):** the real caller chain between file-cap release and model-data destruction (`FD4FileCapDeleter`'s delete slot, `[1447559C8]`); the GX-side purge; the four deleters without an add site; the thread owners of the loader and `GXWorker` pools.
