# Sheet B: bosses (rows B01 to B26, sub-rows a, b, c...)

**Note (2026-11-13, after the merge of the corrected `boss-flags.json`)**: the keys `m38a`, `m38b`, `m51a`, `m51b` and `dregheap` named below are now `wolnir`, `olddemonking`, `halflight`, `midir` and `demonprince`; `namelessking` revives the Nameless King and `friede` Sister Friede (the swaps this sheet found are corrected).

Covers every boss of the base game and both DLC: 26 bosses (mandatory and optional; the Stray Demon is the one named-health-bar
enemy without a fog gate that the data showed; no other mini-boss with a fog gate or named bar was found in the boss maps'
events). Each boss has a summary row (`Bnn`: map, defeat flag, fog gate, bonfires, how to reach it) and sub-rows: `a` the entry
(fog traversal and cutscene), `b`... the phases, the last letter the death.

**Sources.** The game's own data, read read-only: `event/mXX_XX_00_00.emevd.dcx` and `event/common*.emevd.dcx` (dumped with I-0067's
`Get-EmevdDump.ps1`), `map/mapstudio/mXX_XX_00_00.msb.dcx` (SoulsFormats MSB3), `msg/engus/item_dlc1/dlc2.msgbnd.dcx` (the NPC-name FMG).
Dumps: `evidence/I-0139/artifacts/static/bosses/` (tools: `tools/Dump-BossData.ps1`, `tools/boss_join.py`, `tools/gen_bosses_sheet.py`);
extracted bytes (licensed) in `C:/Users/User/Tools/DS3-Decompilation/gamedata-i0138/bosses/` (the gamedata-i0138 folder, used before the
rename). CURATED: the sl2-analyzer `db_ds3/boss_souls.json` (rewards), `boss_route.json` (zones, Stray Demon). RECORD: boss-flags.json,
I-0069 `REMO-CUTSCENES-NATIVE.md` and `emevd-cutscene-calls.txt`, I-0113 RESULT (Iudex Gundyr's F6), LIVE-RUN-NOTES (Iudex's gate

**How the facts were read (STATIC).** Event 970 of `common.emevd` lists the 26 defeat flags. In each map's EMEVD, template 20005500
(flag, bonfire flag, bonfire character entity, bonfire object entity) creates the bonfire that appears after the kill; the
boss's own event family 20005800 (fog, start region), 20005801 and 20005831 (fight flag X05805, heat-up flag X05806, BGM
regions), 20005837 (health bar, NPC id) and 20005840 and 20005841 (fog gate) gives the fog object and the regions; the entity
ids are joined with the MSB parts (positions are in the MSB frame, which is the teleport frame, LIVE-RUN-NOTES). The boss's
name is its character id plus 900000 in the NPC-name FMG (for example c2240 is 902240 Vordt), checked against the bonfire created
by the same template. The Japanese labels of the MSB regions are not reliable (the two bosses of m37_00 carry each other's labels).
Phases: only the BGM regions, the heat-up flags and the FMG names are read; an HP threshold lives in the boss AI scripts, which
were not read, so every phase's behaviour is INFERRED from common knowledge unless a row says STATIC.

(registered flags only), F4 invincible, F5 revive again, F6 kill. A map argument such as `m40_00` revives all the bosses of the map.

the arena with a boss who does not fight (I-0113 tranche 10d): cross the fog from the nearest bonfire instead.

## Gaps

- **Rows in the Travel list.** The tab is known (R11-PLAN section 12) but a bonfire's row is not: BonfireWarpParam's order column
  puts the High Wall's Oceiros bonfire fifth as R11-PLAN does, but disagrees with R11-PLAN for Farron Keep, Demon Ruins and the Grand
  Archives (by one to two rows), so no row is claimed. Smallest tool: one screenshot per tab (`i132-r11c0-eta-tabs`) read once into a table.
- **Phases and HP thresholds** are not read: they live in the bosses' AI scripts (Lua in `script/`, not extracted). Smallest tool:
  extract and read the `*.luabnd` goals of the 25 boss characters; until then a phase is reached by a real fight with F4, or
  by the heat-up flag (X05806) set through `witness-flag.txt` (one id per file; that the AI reads it is INFERRED, untested).
- **F6** takes only a character whose HP maximum is up to 20,000 (code comment): any boss above that is skipped (OPEN per boss:
  NpcParam rows not read). I-0113 saw F6 write 0 HP without a death only while Iudex was not yet fighting. Smallest tool: an F6 variant
  with a larger cap, or a one-hit kill of the named entity id.
- **Victory banner text**: the banner is drawn art (`MENU_VICTORY_ACHIEVED`), not in the FMGs; its wording and the per-boss variants stay INFERRED.
- **Cutscene roles** (intro, mid-fight, post-kill) are read as calls and the flags they test or set, not played: the I-0069 note stands
  that no native run has played a Remo cutscene. Smallest tool: `witness-flag.txt` setting the intro's seen-flag to 0 (for example
  13000801 for Vordt) and walking in.
- **Teleport into an arena** is not offered: the player-start parts nearest to each gate are listed, no arena part exists
  (teleports go to `c0000_*` parts, never to a piece's origin).
- **Bosses whose entity is not in the MSB** (Crystal Sage, Abyss Watchers, Deacons, Pontiff, Dragonslayer Armour, Nameless King, Friede,
  Gravetender, Demon Prince, Halflight, Gael) are spawned by the events: their arena position is the start region's, not read from a part.
- **Stray Demon**: flag and fog (none within 45 m) unconfirmed; **Halflight**, the Ancient Wyvern and the Soul of Cinder create no bonfire
  by template 20005500; no soul item for Iudex, the Wyvern, Halflight and the Gravetender in the curated data (OPEN).
- **Mini-bosses**: the search covered only the boss maps' events (templates 20005800 to 20005841 and 20005837); a named bar without
  those templates would be missed. Smallest tool: scan all map EMEVDs for templates 20005837 and 20005839.
