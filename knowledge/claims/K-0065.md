# K-0065 — Exact character-runtime ownership and manager dispatch spine

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

On Dark Souls III 1.15.2.0 / BuildID 10167187, exact RTTI, constructors,
vtable slots and read-side consumers establish the following reusable
character-runtime architecture:

- `FieldInsBase -> ChrIns -> PlayerIns|EnemyIns`;
- `ChrIns+0x1F90` owns a `0x200` module aggregate with 26 constructor-proved
  populated slots, including TimeAct at slot 2, behavior at slot 5 and physics
  at slot 13;
- `ChrIns+0x50` owns a `ChrCtrl` derivative: `PlayerCtrl` for PlayerIns and
  `EnemyCtrl` for EnemyIns;
- global `0x0477FDB8` owns `NS_SPRJ::WorldChrManImp`, whose constructor-proved
  callback family traverses character lists and dispatches `ChrIns` virtual
  stage adapters;
- one confirmed manager-to-slot-29 edge is
  `0x008DFD60 -> 0x008915A0 -> ChrIns vslot 29`, selecting PlayerIns override
  `0x008C8550` or the EnemyIns-inherited common implementation `0x0089CFA0`.

Within `PlayerCtrl`, LTF-side slot 27 (`0x005E57F0`) and TRTF slot 28
(`0x005E6580`) are separate virtual methods with separate state. EnemyCtrl has
distinct slot-27/28 implementations (`0x005D1B70`/`0x005D2280`) while both
derivatives share ChrCtrl slot 31 (`0x005C9DC0`). This structural intersection
does not merge the corrections or transfer accepted player TRTF behavior to
the enemy recurrence.

## Limits

- Original scheduler phase names and the complete ordering/exclusivity of
  callbacks remain unknown.
- The 143 whole-listing syntactic `+0xE8` call sites are not typed evidence;
  only the constructor-proved WorldChrManImp path is promoted.
- Thirty-eight aggregate slots lack a common-constructor store; no absence or
  unused-slot claim is made.
- Individual physics fields retain their bounded evidence names where original
  semantics are not proved.
- This static architecture claim does not broaden LTF/TRTF validation, modify
  either correction or establish whole-C4 correctness.
