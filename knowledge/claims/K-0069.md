# K-0069 — Typed game-flow clients of the SPRJ timeline

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact-build RTTI and constructor paths establish a typed game-flow scheduling
boundary over the global SPRJ timeline.

`NS_SPRJ::GameMan` owns an embedded exact
`SprjEzUpdateTask<SprjEzTask, GameMan>` at `+0xB98`. Its bootstrap stores the
singleton at global `0x0475AC00` and registers that task in exact named group
`0x09 GameMan`. The constructor-proved task owner and callback are at `+0xBB0`
and `+0xBB8`; callback `0x0062C010` consumes the scheduled context including
the live delta at context `+0x08` in a bounded path.

Separately, exact `NS_SPRJ::TaskItemAdapter -> TaskItem` wraps a
`NS_SPRJ::Task` pointer at `+0x20` and owns an embedded
`SprjEzUpdateTask<SprjEzTask, TaskItemAdapter>` at `+0x28`. The adapter registers
the caller-selected numeric group at priority 0, dispatches wrapped Task
virtual slot 2 while its numeric state is 1 or 2, and unregisters/releases
itself when numeric state 3 is observed.

Concrete constructors bind this bridge to `InGameStayStep`, `InGameStep` and
`MoveMapStep` in exact groups `0x18`, `0x19` and `0x1B`. Direct step-task forms
bind `InGameMenuStep`, `FrpgNetConnectManStep` and `FrpgNetConnectStep` to exact
groups `0x47`, `0x4C` and `0x4D`. Thus in-game and network-flow classes are
distinct scheduled clients of the shared timeline rather than evidence of one
merged flow object.

## Limits

- Numeric states 1/2/3 are observed behavior gates; their original enum names
  and all transition producers are unknown.
- Group `0x07 GameFlowStep` is not yet bound to an exact object.
- Numeric group IDs do not prove serial execution or worker affinity.
- `InGameMenuStep` is proved on group `0x47`; this is not generalized to the
  separate `0x1D TaskLineIdx_InGame_InGameMenuStep` group.
- The claim is static architecture only. It changes no LTF/TRTF logic and makes
  no new gameplay or timing-correctness claim.
