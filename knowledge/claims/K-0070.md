# K-0070 — Game-flow world owner and location scheduling boundary

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact-build RTTI and constructors establish two distinct adjacent branches from
game flow into world/location architecture.

Exact `InGameStep` embeds `WorldInfoOwner` at `+0x190`. Its RTTI chain is
`WorldInfoOwner -> WorldRes -> WorldInfo -> DLNonCopyable`. The inherited header
contains an area count/root at `+0x08/+0x10` and block count/root at
`+0x18/+0x20`. Exact `WorldMapManImp`, `WorldObjManImp`, `WorldPointManImp`,
`WorldSfxManImp`, `WorldSoundManImp` and `WorldHitManImp` constructors consume
that same header and materialize subsystem-specific area and block records.
The examined `InGameStep` initializer directly constructs global exact
`WorldObjManImp` at `0x0475C170` from the embedded owner.

Separately, the engine step bootstrap constructs exact
`SprjFD4LocationStep -> SprjStepTask -> FD4StepTaskBase -> ... ->
FD4ComponentBase` and registers it in exact group `0x25 LocationStep`. The step
owns exact rabbit update tasks at `+0xC8` and `+0xF8`, with callbacks
`0x00D1C870` and `0x00D1C820`, registered in exact groups
`0x26 LocationUpdate_PrePhysics` and `0x27 LocationUpdate_PostCloth`.
Both callbacks consume global exact `SprjFD4LocationImp` at `0x04791BD0`.

Exact `FieldArea` is an additional independent client whose embedded update
task is registered in exact group `0x24 AdhocHavokClothPostUpdate`.

## Proof basis

- Exact RTTI in `world-location-rtti.json` and `world-manager-rtti.json`.
- Read-only/no-analysis Ghidra exports under
  `evidence/I-0015/static/world-location-boundary/`.
- Exact timeline IDs and names in the deterministic engine timeline tables.
- Canonical synthesis in `WORLD-LOCATION-BOUNDARY.md` and deterministic
  validator version 4.

## Limits

- Only `WorldObjManImp` is directly tied to the examined `InGameStep`
  initializer. Peer world-manager constructors prove the same consumer contract
  but not a common lifetime.
- Area/block descriptor fields beyond the counts and roots are unnamed.
- Location callback work-item types and their virtual-slot `0x40` semantics are
  unknown.
- Numeric groups do not prove simple serial execution or worker affinity.
- `FieldArea` group placement does not prove universal terrain or collision
  ownership and does not modify the TRTF interpretation.
- Static architecture only; no live process was accessed and LTF/TRTF are
  unchanged.
