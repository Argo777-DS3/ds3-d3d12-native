# K-0071 — World topology and resource-descriptor producer

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact-build RTTI and constructor stores close the producer side of the
`InGameStep -> WorldInfoOwner` boundary. The examined `InGameStep` initializer
either derives one map identifier from its own map fields or derives an ordered
set from a bounded source object at `+0x180`; both paths converge on
`0x0061F9C0 -> 0x0061F710`.

The common producer resets the owner, normalizes the input identifiers and
groups them by one exact internal byte. It constructs one exact
`WorldAreaInfo` and `WorldAreaRes` per group, and one exact `WorldBlockInfo` and
`WorldBlockRes` per normalized input. Exact fixed-capacity owner arrays are:

- 20 `WorldAreaInfo` records at `+0x10`, stride `0x38`;
- 32 `WorldBlockInfo` records at `+0x20`, stride `0x70`;
- 20 `WorldAreaRes` records at `+0x12A8`, stride `0x108`;
- 32 `WorldBlockRes` records at `+0x12C0`, stride `0x438`.

The paired info/resource counts are written identically. Constructor stores
bind parent, peer, owner, global index, area-local index and contiguous block
ranges. `InGameStep` then sets the topology-ready byte and refreshes eligible
block encoded identifiers from exact global `GameMan` before constructing peer
world-manager consumers in the examined initializer.

## Proof basis

- Exact descriptor RTTI in `world-descriptor-rtti.json`.
- Read-only/no-analysis Ghidra exports under
  `evidence/I-0015/static/world-streaming-producer/`.
- Exact `InGameStep`, `WorldInfo`, `WorldRes` and manager-construction edges
  already retained under `world-location-boundary/`.
- Canonical synthesis in `WORLD-TOPOLOGY-PRODUCER.md` and deterministic
  validator version 5.

## Limits

- The source object at `InGameStep+0x180`, the input-branch predicate and the
  map-identifier components retain unknown original names.
- The grouping byte is structurally confirmed; “area key” is descriptive and
  only SUPPORTED.
- Exact `WorldAreaRes`/`WorldBlockRes` identities prove a resource-record
  layer, not disk/package I/O, asynchronous streaming, asset completion,
  visibility, unloading or complete activation semantics.
- The observed producer-to-manager order is bounded to this initializer and
  does not prove global timeline order or thread affinity.
- Static architecture only; no live process was accessed and LTF/TRTF are
  unchanged.
