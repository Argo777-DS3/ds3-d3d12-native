# K-0072 — InGameStep and WorldObjManImp lifetime contract

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `InGameStep` initialization and teardown establish a bounded lifetime
contract between its embedded `WorldInfoOwner` and global exact
`NS_SPRJ::WorldObjManImp`.

After topology production, `InGameStep` constructs `WorldObjManImp` with the
embedded owner and publishes it at global `0x0475C170`. The manager retains the
owner, copies its active area/block counts and creates one exact
`WorldAreaObj` per `WorldAreaInfo` plus one exact `WorldBlockObj` per
`WorldBlockInfo`.

On teardown, `InGameStep` invokes exact `WorldObjManImp` virtual slot zero,
frees the manager allocation and clears its global. The manager destructor
removes all active block-object and area-object records. Only afterward does
`InGameStep` clear `WorldInfo+0x28` and reset/destruct the four owner-side
info/resource arrays.

## Proof basis

- Exact `WorldObjManImp` RTTI and constructor/destructor exports.
- Exact `InGameStep` initializer `0x008EA490` and teardown `0x008E9EE0`.
- Exact vtable slot-zero target `0x00680020`, ready-clear helper `0x00620020`
  and descriptor reset `0x0061FED0`.
- Canonical synthesis in `WORLD-OBJECT-LIFETIME.md` and deterministic validator
  version 6.

## Limits

- The construction/destruction order is confirmed only for the examined
  `InGameStep` pair; it is not a global scheduler or thread-order claim.
- Manager activation states and exact update group remain unknown.
- Object-record payloads and the 72-function direct-global consumer surface are
  not exhaustively classified.
- Peer world-manager lifetimes are not inferred from this one exact consumer.
- No disk I/O or full streaming lifecycle is claimed. Static architecture only;
  no live process was accessed and LTF/TRTF are unchanged.
