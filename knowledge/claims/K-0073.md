# K-0073 — InGameStep typed service composition

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `InGameStep` initialization and teardown own paired global lifetimes for
four additional exact services:

- `MapItemManImpl` at `0x04769240`, allocation `0x158`;
- `MiniMapImp` at `0x0478CCB8`, allocation `0xA0`;
- `SprjObjImp` at `0x047904A8`, allocation `0x38`;
- `WindWorldImp` at `0x047914D0`, allocation `0x78`.

Each type is bound by exact MSVC RTTI and constructor vtable store. The
initializer lazily allocates, constructs and publishes each object; the paired
teardown invokes the exact virtual destruction entry, releases the allocation
and clears the same global. `SprjObjImp` uses vtable slot one while the other
three use slot zero, so their destruction layouts remain explicitly distinct.

## Proof basis

- Exact RTTI and virtual targets in `ingame-service-rtti.json`.
- Exact constructors and destructor targets under
  `evidence/I-0015/static/ingame-service-composition/`.
- Exact `InGameStep` initializer `0x008EA490` and teardown `0x008E9EE0`.
- Canonical synthesis in `INGAME-SERVICE-COMPOSITION.md` and deterministic
  validator version 7.

## Limits

- Paired ownership does not prove activation state, update group, dependency
  order or thread affinity.
- `SprjObjImp` responsibilities remain partial and the wind service is not
  assigned exclusively to rendering, physics or effects.
- Other globals in the same initializer are excluded until a unique top-level
  RTTI identity is recovered. Exact embedded packet types do not name their
  host service.
- Static architecture only; no renderer redesign or live access occurred and
  LTF/TRTF are unchanged.
