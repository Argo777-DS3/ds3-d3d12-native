# K-0074 — MiniMap typed producer/consumer boundary

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact RTTI and bounded call paths place global `NS_SPRJ::MiniMapImp` between
three established architectural producers/consumers. World/location refresh
feeds it a resolved location identifier; exact `WorldBlockRes` destruction
coordinates its mode-zero teardown; exact `MoveMapStep` callback `0x008F9AD0`
feeds it current-player state reached through `WorldChrManImp+0x80`. Its
resource state in turn participates in constructing exact `MiniMapBlock` and
`MiniMapLayer` objects. One activation and two additional transition paths
share the resource layout and MiniMap calls but remain `SUPPORTED` because
their owner is not typed by a canonical caller/reference.

The selected exact-global scan also bounds the narrower `SprjObjImp` surface:
its two examined non-lifecycle callbacks are direct `RET` stubs in this build,
so no gameplay responsibility is inferred from that type name.

## Proof basis

- Exact service/global operand inventory in `service-global-operands.csv`.
- Exact `MiniMapImp`, `MoveMapStep`, `WorldBlockRes`, `MiniMapBlock` and
  `MiniMapLayer` RTTI.
- Bounded read-only Ghidra exports under
  `evidence/I-0015/static/ingame-service-composition/`.
- Canonical synthesis in `MINIMAP-CONSUMER-BOUNDARY.md` and deterministic
  validator version 8.

## Limits

- The minimap does not own world topology, player state or `WorldBlockRes`.
- Numeric block-resource modes and minimap field names remain unknown. Only
  mode-zero teardown is exactly bound to `WorldBlockRes`; the activation and
  two other modes retain `SUPPORTED` ownership.
- `MoveMapStep` callback identity does not establish timeline group, execution
  order or thread affinity.
- No rendering API, disk I/O, complete streaming or live-runtime claim is made.
  LTF and TRTF are unchanged.
