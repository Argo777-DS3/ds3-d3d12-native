# K-0075 — WindWorld character-attachment boundary

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact RTTI and virtual dispatch establish a typed character-to-world wind
chain. `ChrWindSlot` is an exact `ChrAttachSysSlot` specialization whose slot
three update consumes a `ChrIns` attachment argument, resolves two transforms,
constructs exact `WindRegion`, allocates exact `WindIns`, retains it at
`ChrWindSlot+0x28` and registers it in exact global `WindWorldImp`. Slot two,
invalid-id update and destruction remove the same instance before release.

Separately, exact `MoveMapStep` callback `0x008F9AD0` calls `0x008FC850`, which
forwards its tagged float input to exact `WindWorldImp`. The final jump target
is outside the recovered main image body and remains `UNKNOWN`.

## Proof basis

- Exact RTTI/hierarchy and vtable stores in `chr-wind-slot-rtti-search.json`,
  `wind-client-rtti.json` and `wind-object-rtti.json`.
- Exact service lifetime from K-0073.
- Bounded read-only Ghidra exports under
  `evidence/I-0015/static/ingame-service-composition/`.
- Canonical synthesis in `WIND-CHARACTER-BOUNDARY.md` and deterministic
  validator version 9.

## Limits

- Numeric attachment/descriptor codes and individual transform field names
  remain unknown.
- The unresolved `MoveMapStep` dispatch target prevents an internal update or
  timing-formula claim.
- The service is not assigned exclusively to renderer, physics, simulation or
  effects, and no live-runtime claim is made. LTF/TRTF are unchanged.
