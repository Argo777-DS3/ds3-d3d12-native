# K-0079 — World-block transition coordination

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `WorldBlockRes` mode-zero destruction reads its peer
`WorldBlockInfo+0x20` global block index and fans removal out to exact
`WorldChrManImp`, `WorldObjManImp` and `MiniMapImp`. Their bounded consumers
respectively clear a `0x138`-stride character block record, reset the indexed
exact `WorldBlockObj` at stride `0x40288`, and remove the matching exact
`MiniMapBlock` representation.

## Proof basis

- Exact `WorldBlockRes` RTTI and slot-zero destructor chain.
- Exact singleton/global identities already established by K-0065, K-0072 and
  K-0073.
- Read-only Ghidra exports for `0x0061E3D0`, `0x008DB270`, `0x00682BB0` and
  `0x00A39700`.
- Deterministic validator version 13.

## Supported inverse

`0x0061DF10` uses the same complete `WorldBlockRes` layout and block index,
then calls inverse preparation functions `0x008DB250`, `0x00682B70` and
`0x00A39620`. This preparation identity remains `SUPPORTED` because its typed
direct caller is not recovered.

## Limits

- Other service and peer-manager calls are not promoted beyond their existing
  evidence status.
- Numeric transition modes, ordering, thread affinity, streaming completion,
  visibility and renderer ownership remain unknown.
- Static-only; no live process, installed game file, LTF or TRTF change.
