# K-0080 — World-area aggregation negative

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `WorldAreaRes` destruction conditionally dispatches the peer
`WorldAreaInfo+0x18` area index through `0x00612340`; it does not read the
area-resource child block count/root or iterate `WorldBlockRes` records in the
examined path. The exact `WorldObjManImp` area target is a return stub. Eleven
adjacent preparation/removal targets across the same six world-manager method
families are also exact return-only bodies.

## Proof basis

- Exact `WorldAreaRes` RTTI slot-zero target `0x0060FAA0`.
- Read-only Ghidra exports for destructor `0x0060F5D0`, coordinator
  `0x00612340` and twelve exact return-only targets.
- K-0079 block-transition boundary and deterministic validator version 14.

## Limits

- Only `WorldObjManImp` has an exact global/type pairing in this callback
  family; the map/point/SFX/sound/hit pairings remain SUPPORTED.
- Other services called by the area coordinator remain untyped and may own
  area-level state.
- No assertion is made about alternate activation paths, ordering, streaming
  completion or renderer ownership.
- Static-only; no live process, installed game file, LTF or TRTF change.
