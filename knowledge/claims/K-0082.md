# K-0082 — PlayerIns manipulator ownership

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

The exact `PlayerIns` constructor's general manipulator path uses a
configuration selector and stores the factory result at `PlayerIns+0x58`.
Selector 1 allocates and constructs exact `NS_SPRJ::PadManipulator`, derived
from `NS_SPRJ::ChrManipulator`; paired `PlayerIns` destruction virtually
deletes and clears that pointer. This closes a typed character-to-manipulator
ownership boundary.

## Proof basis

[Canonical bounded reconstruction](../../evidence/I-0015/static/input-character-boundary/PLAYER-MANIPULATOR-BOUNDARY.md)
and its exact-build RTTI/vtable and read-only Ghidra exports.

## Limits

Selector 6 has a separate constructor path; the selector-1 object is not
asserted to represent every live player. `SprjPad`, `PadStep`, hardware input,
button/axis mappings and sampling order are not joined to this owner yet.
Static-only; no LTF/TRTF change or game launch.
