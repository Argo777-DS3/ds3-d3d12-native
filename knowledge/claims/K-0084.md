# K-0084 — Typed camera task registration

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `SprjCameraStep`, derived through `SprjStepTask<SprjCameraStep>` and
FD4 step-task types, is constructed and registered by the engine task
composer through the established step-task helper in timeline group
`0x43 CameraStep`. Exact `SprjCameraImp` and `CameraMan` are separately
constructed/published typed services; neither has a proved ownership or
read-side edge to the scheduled step in the bounded evidence.

## Proof basis

[Canonical camera boundary](../../evidence/I-0015/static/camera-schedule-boundary/CAMERA-SCHEDULE-BOUNDARY.md),
exact RTTI/vtable tables, direct task registration call and read-only Ghidra
exports linked there.

## Limits

Group membership is not execution order or thread affinity. Global address
adjacency does not type a pointer. No camera-update formula, view transform,
rendering or gameplay-timing claim is made. No game launch or LTF/TRTF change.
