# K-0085 — Camera-service read into current-player context

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

One exact `SprjCameraImp` global reader at `0x00EC88B0` obtains the
implementation's `+0x08` child, copies two 64-byte child-state blocks, then
passes two pointers into those copies to `0x00EC69B0` with the exact current
`PlayerIns` selected through `WorldChrManImp+0x80`. The receiving routine
builds a bounded player-context request and forwards it to a separate
untyped service.

## Proof basis

[Camera/player read boundary](../../evidence/I-0015/static/camera-schedule-boundary/CAMERA-PLAYER-READ-BOUNDARY.md),
exact-global operand inventory, direct call/field reads, and read-only Ghidra
exports linked there.

## Limits

This is a typed camera-service-to-player-context data handoff, not proof
that the camera changes movement or player state. The child type, value
names, request-service purpose, caller's timeline group and scheduled
`SprjCameraStep` relationship remain UNKNOWN. No LTF/TRTF change.
