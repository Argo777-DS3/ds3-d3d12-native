# K-0086 — Distinct typed file/resource schedule clients

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `SprjFileStep` and `SprjResStep` are separately constructed and
registered in named timeline groups `0x03 FileStep` and `0x04 ResStep`.
`SprjFileStep` retains an exact `FD4FailoverAllocator` pointer and embeds a
typed `SprjEzUpdateTask` targeting itself. Its separate `+0x108` object has
a paired release path.

## Proof basis

[File/resource schedule boundary](../../evidence/I-0015/static/resource-schedule-boundary/FILE-RESOURCE-SCHEDULE-BOUNDARY.md),
exact-hash RTTI, task-composer calls and read-only Ghidra exports linked
there.

## Limits

Group names do not establish task execution order. The selected callback
bodies are undefined in the canonical project; no FileStep-to-ResStep
handoff, `ResMan` relation, disk I/O or streaming behavior is proved.
