# K-0089 — Typed world-area resource to sound-bank repository release

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

`SprjSoundImp`, `SprjFD4SoundImp` and `SprjFD4MobankRepositoryImp` are
separately typed and published by a bounded sound initializer chain.
Exact `WorldAreaRes` destruction calls a release routine that resolves
five area-keyed names against the exact Mobank repository and
conditionally releases found records.

## Proof basis

[Sound service/world-area boundary](../../evidence/I-0015/static/sound-boundary/SOUND-SERVICE-WORLD-AREA-BOUNDARY.md),
exact RTTI and constructor/global stores, typed `WorldAreaRes` destructor,
repository-lookup/release bodies, and read-only Ghidra exports linked there.

## Limits

The destructor passes a `SprjSoundImp` pointer but the called routine
does not use that argument; repository operations use a separate global.
The inverse preparation caller remains SUPPORTED. No step-to-service
execution edge, I/O, bank unload or sound emission is claimed.
