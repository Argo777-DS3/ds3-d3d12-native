# K-0090 — FrpgNetManImp owns two distinct scheduled network clients

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `FrpgNetManImp` is directly allocated/published at global
`0x04756E48`. It embeds a typed update task registered in exact group
`0x0C FrpgNetMan`, while a separately constructed exact `FrpgNetSysStep`
is registered at manager `+0x10` in `0x4A GameFlowFrpgNet_Sys`.
Previously proved connection steps occupy `0x4C` and `0x4D`.

## Proof basis

[Network manager schedule boundary](../../evidence/I-0015/static/network-boundary/NETWORK-MANAGER-SCHEDULE-BOUNDARY.md),
exact RTTI, constructor stores, publisher, registration operands and
timeline descriptors linked there.

## Limits

Group names do not prove execution order or online behavior. Manager
shutdown, lobby-group client, protocol and transport remain UNKNOWN.
No live process or network session was accessed.
