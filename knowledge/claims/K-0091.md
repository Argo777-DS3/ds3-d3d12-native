Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

`0x04752F80`, and `FieldArea` teardown destroys and clears it. The
manager retains exact `SprjEventDropItemInfo` at `+0x28`; exact
`EcTestEventRegionDrop` consumes that child to compute a bounded
condition result bit. A Lua proxy is retained through a separate
peer root in the same initializer, without a proved direct join.
