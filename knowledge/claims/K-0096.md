# K-0096 — Typed AI section/mesh registration in Havok-AI world

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

A bounded producer constructs exact `SprjHkAiSection` and may attach
exact `SprjHkAiNaviMesh` at section `+0xA0` with a back-pointer.
It passes the section to a keyed insert method on the already exact
`SprjHkAiWorld` child of the separate wrapper. A duplicate-key branch
returns without insertion; a failed caller branch destroys the section.

exact RTTI/vtables, constructor stores, conditional attachment,
