# K-0100 — Exact EVD file-cap submission to SprjFileImp

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `SprjEvdFileCap` constructors install vtable `0x2799208`.
`0x00503120` allocates the cap and passes it with a name descriptor to
the virtual slot-3 surface of exact `SprjFileImp`; one caller supplies the
literal `event:/m99_99_99_99.emevd` and retains the selected result
at `+0xC8`. Exact `SprjFileImp` is constructed and published at global
`0x1447559C8`, and its slot-3 adapter reaches common cap
selection/collection code at `0x023490B0`.

## Proof basis

[Cycle-5 file-cap owner boundary](../../evidence/I-0016/static/evd-file-cap-owner/EVD-FILE-CAP-OWNER-BOUNDARY.md),
exact-build RTTI, direct decoded constructors/callers, and the bounded
pseudo-instruction dump of the virtual adapter.

## Limits

This is a typed file-manager capability submission/selection edge.
It does not establish `SprjFileStep` or `SprjResStep` activation, physical
I/O, completion, readiness, a typed event-manager handoff or a world
resource. The exact selected-result ownership and collection semantics
remain `UNKNOWN`.
