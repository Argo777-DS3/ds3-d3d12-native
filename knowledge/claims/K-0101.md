# K-0101 — Exact EVD resource cap composes EVD, EDF and ELD results

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

On the new-object path, exact `SprjEvdResCap` is initialized at
`0x00E0B5F0` with an untyped subordinate record at `+0x60`, an indexed
EVD-repository result array at `+0x70`, and EDF/ELD-repository results
at `+0x78/+0x80`. Selected result `+0x60` pointers are attached to
the subordinate record's indexed `+0x38` array and `+0x48/+0x50`
fields. The three exact repositories are the manager-published types
already established by K-0099.

## Proof basis

[Cycle-7 composition boundary](../../evidence/I-0016/static/evd-rescap-composition/EVD-RESCAP-COMPOSITION-BOUNDARY.md),
exact-build RTTI, decoded initializer call/stores and helper bodies.

## Limits

The EVD lookup helper jumps into protected code; returned member
types and contents are not established. The subordinate record is
untyped. This structural fan-in does not establish physical loading,
readiness, completion, event-script execution, or a typed transfer to
`SprjEventManImp` or a world resource. The selected post-initializer
decompilation contains bad instruction data and is excluded.

The [cycle-8 consumer test](../../evidence/I-0016/static/evd-rescap-consumer/EVD-RESCAP-CONSUMER-BOUNDARY.md)
corroborates partial teardown of the subordinate record and indexed EVD
results. An earlier protected teardown call prevents a claim about
EDF/ELD result release; no typed event/world consumer was recovered.
