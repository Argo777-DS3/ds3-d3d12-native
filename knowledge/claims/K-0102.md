# K-0102 — AI section handle cleanup reaches exact file service

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

The untyped per-area source at `+0x40` supplies a reference-counted handle to the I-0016 `SprjHkAiSection` entry path. At final handle release, its wrapper passes each non-null one of two held pointers to virtual slot 6 of exact `SprjFileImp` and clears them. This is a typed cleanup-side file-service boundary, not an origin/type assertion for the held resource.

## Proof basis

[I-0017 AI handle/file boundary](../../evidence/I-0017/static/ai-handle-file-boundary/AI-HANDLE-FILE-BOUNDARY.md), the decoded `0x00DC7A20 -> 0x00DB40F0 -> 0x00DB4590` and `0x00DC2F50 -> 0x00DB4550 -> 0x00DB4160 -> 0x00DB3FA0` chains, and K-0100's exact `SprjFileImp` global identity.

## Limits

The upstream producer of the per-area `+0x40` source, the two held pointer types, handle payload and exact NVM repository/world-resource origin remain `UNKNOWN`. The lookup's structural resemblance to NVM resource code is insufficient to type its array. Slot 6 handoff does not establish physical I/O or completion.
