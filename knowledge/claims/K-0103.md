# K-0103 — Exact SoundStep callbacks reach the Mobank repository

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact game-flow `SoundStep` retains a two-entry callback table. Its indexed dispatch invokes callbacks `0x00905980` and `0x009059D0`; both pass exact `SprjSoundImp` global to wrappers that ignore that argument and instead issue selected requests/queries against exact `SprjFD4MobankRepositoryImp` global. The second callback conditionally sets step state `-1` based on the repository record predicate.

## Proof basis

[I-0017 sound callback boundary](../../evidence/I-0017/static/sound-step-service/SOUND-STEP-SERVICE-BOUNDARY.md), decoded constructor/dispatcher/callbacks, hash-matched table, K-0088's step identity and K-0089's separate service/repository publications.

## Limits

No direct callback edge from distinct `SprjSoundStep` (group `0x36`) or `WorldSoundManImp` is established. Repository request/query structure does not prove disk loading, readiness, audio emission, update order or timing.
