# K-0104 — Havok manager task callbacks consume exact SprjClothImp

Status: `CONFIRMED`

## Claim

Exact `FrpgHavokManImp` constructor installs callbacks in seven embedded update tasks. Its registration path conditionally constructs and publishes exact `SprjClothImp` at global `0x144794EB0`, and five of the installed callbacks directly read that typed global and pass its records or children to selected operations. This joins the manager-owned scheduled task surface to a separate exact cloth service.

## Proof basis

[I-0017 Havok/cloth callback boundary](../../evidence/I-0017/static/havok-cloth-callback/HAVOK-CLOTH-CALLBACK-BOUNDARY.md), exact constructor callback stores, K-0093 task registration, selected callback exports and hash-matched `SprjClothImp` RTTI.

## Limits

The untyped `0x144964978` global used by several callees is distinct from the exact cloth object. No task execution, thread order, frequency, physical cloth effect, protected callee meaning or timing behavior is established by the static callback chain.
