# K-0045 — Runtime identity of spatial-exchange owners

Status: `CONFIRMED` for the 15 owner instances retained in the complete prefix
of session `20260920T095812Z-pid27644`.

## Claim

Read-only RTTI inspection after the observer had stopped and restored the game
resolved all 15 spatial-exchange owners as
`NS_SPRJ::SprjBehaviorCharacterProxyDriver`. Their `owner+0x90` referents are
one `NS_SPRJ::PlayerIns` and 14 `NS_SPRJ::EnemyIns`; all 15 reachable
`player+0x1F90 -> modules+0x28` objects are
`NS_SPRJ::SprjChrBehaviorModule`.

The PlayerIns-backed owner uses only the unscaled-delta regime. All ten owners
with `30 * delta` forward entries are EnemyIns-backed and occur for six or seven
captured forward entries. Four other EnemyIns-backed owners, plus the player
owner, persist across all 192 updates and use only unscaled delta. Thus the
scaled regime is bounded to transient EnemyIns-backed instances in this prefix,
while EnemyIns type alone is insufficient to select it.

## Limits

The RTTI read occurred after capture from the still-live exact-build process,
using query/read access only. It identifies these instances and object-chain
types; it does not assign a gameplay role to the transient enemies, explain the
caller-side selection of `30 * delta`, prove lifetime outside the retained
prefix, or make the overflowed target165 trial acceptable for cross-rate use.
