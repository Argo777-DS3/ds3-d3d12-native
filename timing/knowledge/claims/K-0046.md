# K-0046 — Spatial exchange is a character-proxy driver virtual pair

Status: `CONFIRMED` for Dark Souls III 1.15.2.0 / BuildID 10167187.

## Claim

Exact-build RTTI and vtable inspection identifies `00DD3850` and `00DD3B50` as
consecutive slots 6 and 7 of
`NS_SPRJ::SprjBehaviorCharacterProxyDriver`. The driver constructor installs
this table and clears `+0x90`; initialization writes the owning PlayerIns or
EnemyIns pointer to `+0x90` and attaches the driver at
`SprjChrBehaviorModule+0x50`.

This binds K-0042's forward/reverse functions and K-0045's live owner chain to
one exact class and module attachment path.

## Limits

the image. Static slot-offset search cannot select their actual dispatchers or
explain why a transient subset receives `30 * delta` on forward entry. The new
