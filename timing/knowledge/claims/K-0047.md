# K-0047 — Stable spatial blocks have a bounded transform layout

Status: `CONFIRMED` for the accepted target60 stable-owner slot; independently
reproduced at row level in the complete prefix of the rejected target165 run.

## Claim

Across all 360 accepted target60 stable-slot exchanges, selected and
reverse-current 48-byte blocks form three float4 groups with these invariants:

- lane3 is exactly1;
- lanes4–7 have unit norm, lanes4/6 are zero, and
  `2 * atan2(lane5,lane7)` matches sampled player angle in360/360 updates;
- lanes8–10 are within `1e-5` of1 and lane11 is exactly1;
- reverse-current lanes0/2 match sampled player x/y in360/360 updates.

All corresponding invariants and correlations repeat in192/192 stable-slot
rows of the rejected target165 prefix. The result supports a layout of three
float4 groups consistent with translation plus homogeneous lane, unit
orientation, and unit scale. Lane1 does not match the existing sampled z field,
so the full coordinate convention is not assigned.

The forward-derived block is structurally distinct: it preserves lane3=1,
unit lanes4–7 and unit lanes8–10, but lane11 is0 in all552 examined stable-slot
rows and its identity-like orientation does not match player angle.
